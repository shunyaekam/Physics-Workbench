# Discovery docket

**One analytic special case of an externally posed conjecture.** The [phase-invariant light paper](../papers/phase-invariant-wigner.pdf) proves Gagatsos's Conjecture 1 for every photon-number-diagonal state. The conjecture for all single-mode states remains open in the sources reviewed. The earlier Werner studies concern internally posed structural and computational questions.

## From a lead to a result

1. Capture a primary-source locator and the exact claim, including qualifications and equality conditions. Search aliases, equivalent formulations, preprints, later versions, citations, and existing formalizations. Record known special cases and a dated search. Treat a social post as a lead.
2. Give each screened target a stable ID, a scientific consequence, an argument for tractability, and the exact observation that would settle it. Keep finite cases, internal lemmas, and infrastructure benchmarks visibly linked to the parent question. Dedupe new intakes against the active IDs before admitting an adapter.
3. Assign a bounded experiment to a target. Keep seeds, failures, controls, model outputs, and evidence. Rotate if a method stalls. The automated dispatcher currently covers two approved targets. Support-size enumeration gets one round in eight. Extension to a new physics problem needs a scientific dossier and an executable adapter.
4. A candidate gets a source-to-claim map, independent mathematical challenge and a bounded prior-resolution review. Proofs with Lean coverage must name the declaration, commit, toolchain, and axioms. Numerical counterexamples need certified strict separation after error bounds. Revisit the literature immediately before any priority claim.
5. A reviewed full resolution can receive a release tag and an entry here, with links to the target, proof or witness, adversarial review and precise verification scope. Working drafts and numerical searches continue on the private working branch. Promotion is editorial; neither a PDF rebuild nor a passing model critique increments the count.

The model for these steps is Cinna Davis's documented eligibility, significance, research-branch and publication workflow. Davis also accepts reviewed written results with their formalization status disclosed. Their public policy explicitly deprioritizes small parameter variations that have no demonstrated impact on a larger question. ## What the first ten rounds taught us

At round ten, the best four-copy witness (5/7788) had 1, 1, 8 and 1 distinct local index pairs at the respective sites. The five-copy witness (3/110720) had 5, 1, 1, 1 and 1. Both reduce to a one-copy vector times fixed basis factors. Each such factor has strictly positive expectation (1 or 1/2), preserving the one-copy sign. Their small positive scores gave a misleading impression of proximity to a multicopy counterexample. The new arm generates odd triples varying across all sites and admits a candidate only if both parties vary on every site. This necessary scope check for the new arm does not rule out more general factorizations. Historical receipts remain replayable.
