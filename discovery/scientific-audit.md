# Scientific audit of oo research

**25 November 2026.** We audited the nine manuscripts on the public catalogue, the separate cubic-phase certificate, and the status claims attached to the research campaigns. One manuscript gives a credible proposed answer across the full scope of a registered external question. The records reviewed contain **no external referee report accepting any of the nine papers and no end-to-end Lean proof of a headline physical theorem**. These are mathematical results about specified models. None of the papers reports an experimental measurement.

One skeptical objection to transport was too strong. For the source's **unrooted** cost, the same optimal uniform error also holds over all pairs of density matrices. For each coupling $\Pi$, $|\operatorname{Tr}[(C-D)\Pi]|\leq\|C-D\|_{\rm op}$. Taking the infimum over the same set of couplings preserves this additive bound; pure-state pairs already force the matching lower bound. This does not give an error formula for a centered or rooted transport distance. [Definitions in the primary source](https://github.com/ipitchford/quantum-transport-cost-cones/blob/270f5dba795ffd73d271bfde959237b730227c5e/manuscript.tex#L69-L80)

## Four optics manuscripts on one conjecture

The common question concerns the Wigner and Husimi distributions of an ideal single-mode state. The square-root Husimi integral runs over the entire phase plane. Turning a mathematical upper bound into an experimental certificate from finitely many noisy heterodyne samples would also require statistical error and tail control. That step is absent from these papers. [Gagatsos's conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30969)

| Manuscript | Verdict after challenge |
| --- | --- |
| [Phase-invariant light](../papers/phase-invariant-wigner.pdf) | The strongest positive optics result here. The argument covers arbitrary photon-number mixtures, including infinite support, at order one half. The vacuum is the unique case of finite equality in this class. It relies on diagonal structure. A coherent superposition with the same photon-number probabilities can have a different Wigner norm. The reviewer rederived the moment comparison and replayed the arithmetic induction. |
| [Single-rail qubit](../papers/single-rail-wigner.pdf) | All coherences and mixtures on vacuum and one photon are covered. Sampling the Bloch ball, as the source did, cannot establish its boundary. The analytic argument and finite certificate supply that missing step. The one-photon cutoff makes the physical scope narrow. |
| [Rényi-order obstructions](../papers/renyi-obstructions.pdf) | The source anticipated the dilute-cat mechanism. The paper supplies a proof for each fixed order above one half, including the low-energy limit, and a one-photon lower obstruction near 0.288703. The remaining interval is a **necessary** range for a universal bound. Sufficiency throughout it is shown here for the optical qubit and for diagonal mixtures through 144 photons. The unrestricted order-one-half conjecture is open. |
| [Sparse Fock coherence](../papers/sparse-fock-coherence.pdf) | Useful chiefly as a constraint on counterexample searches. Within levels zero through five the proof covers mixtures admitting a decomposition into pure states with support on at most two Fock levels. General states on those six levels can have higher coherence number. The old replay did not bind every printed higher-level table entry to its two amplitude-order receipts. The revised checker reads the printed table and replays both covers. The catalogue edition has 24 excited-level pairs; the research edition has 54. The checker rejected four deliberately corrupted inputs for each edition. [Table checker](../checks/sparse_table.py) |

The separate [cubic-phase certificate](https://github.com/shunyaekam/Physics-Workbench/blob/research/discovery/cubic-strength.md) gives a sufficient interval in the normalized cubic strength, up to $|\kappa|=0.15$, with Gaussian postprocessing. The upper end is a limit of the certificate. It has not been established as the physical threshold. A different weak multimode result follows from an existing short-time Fourier transform bound and is already excluded from discovery credit. [Prior-art decision](https://github.com/shunyaekam/Physics-Workbench/blob/research/checks/multimode-priority.md)

## Two Werner manuscripts on one restricted search

[Coordinate compressions](../papers/coordinate-supports.pdf) has a genuine necessary-and-sufficient criterion in the specified product basis. An odd triangle supplies a negative direction of Schmidt rank **three**. The external distillability question needs rank **at most two**. This explains both the value and the limit of the result: it can prune a witness search, but a failed pruning test cannot witness distillability. The audit replayed the dense physical matrix on all 512 two-copy qutrit supports (97 positive, including the empty set), the threshold comparisons, and five deliberately damaged certificates. [Source on distillability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05726)

[Finite maxima](../papers/finite-maxima.pdf) is a companion verification note. The public table's five values replayed. Its four-symbol row was already implied by the coordinate paper. On re-examination, **every three-copy row follows from the same matching argument**, giving $\max(8,2d)$ for every $d\ge2$. A separate reviewer checked the argument's binary-coordinate case and the possible sizes of the outside matchings. The derivation is included in the note; its priority as a combinatorial fact is unassessed. We grouped the table with the coordinate result and removed covered families from the worker's search queue.

## How the criticism was checked

The reviewers tried to invalidate specific steps and replayed executable checks, including the published Werner maxima, optical certificates, all three paper verification packages and the restricted Lean claims. The transport re-audit tested 1,024 exact Monge arrays and 120 floating-point linear programs. A six-level counterexample defeats the tempting weakening from Monge structure to superadditivity. The same AI reviewer conducted the initial and repeat transport audits; the second pass added new tests. A first-time internal reviewer then read Pitchford’s statement and the counting proof before reading the other reports. They reconstructed the all-marked crossing argument, the coloring step and the Werner bound independently, and found no invalid step. All internal agents share a model family. Agreement is evidence against the concrete mistakes they tested and offers limited protection against a shared blind spot. The source and proof comparison, signed counterexamples and reproducible checkers carry more weight than the number of reviewers.

This review covered the published scientific claims and representative campaign records. It did not repeat billions of circuit edits, establish exhaustive priority, reproduce an experiment, or obtain outside peer review.
