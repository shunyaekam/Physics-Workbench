# Scientific review of the first campaign

Reviewed on November 25, 2026. Eight registered cases produced 24 replayable attempts. Eight used fixed settings and 16 used the locally installed Llama 3.2 3B model to select settings. Two further experiments tested a different family of quantum witnesses. Two open-ended AI research notes were also audited. The campaign establishes reproducibility of the methods and gives finite-instance bounds. It establishes zero new solutions to named open problems.

## Electrostatics

For eleven identical unit charges on a unit sphere, the degree-five search certified a lower bound of 40.4910942205. The model selected degree twelve and the same checker certified 40.499955496. The best certified upper bound across the three runs was 40.59645050819091. Cambridge lists a configuration energy of 40.5964505. The remaining gap is about 0.096495. Higher polynomial degree accounts for the observed improvement over our baseline. A deterministic degree sweep could find it too. This experiment has no claim to the strongest published lower bound. [Cambridge Cluster Database](https://www-wales.ch.cam.ac.uk/~wales/CCD/Thomson/table.html)

The upper bound comes from explicit charge positions. The stored integers describe rational points on the sphere via inverse stereographic projection. For every pair, the checker uses integer square root and outward rounding to enclose the reciprocal distance. Adding those intervals encloses the energy of this configuration, whose upper endpoint bounds the global minimum from above.

For the lower bound let x be the dot product of two unit position vectors, r their chord distance, and h(x) a polynomial expanded in Legendre polynomials with coefficients cₗ. Search fits h on a numerical grid. The checker first establishes that every coefficient with index at least one is nonnegative. It then establishes 1 − r h(1 − r²/2) ≥ 0 on the full interval 0 ≤ r ≤ 2 with exact rational Bernstein coefficients and interval subdivision. By the spherical harmonic addition theorem, the sum of Pₗ(xᵢ·xⱼ) over all ordered pairs is nonnegative. Subtracting the self terms and halving therefore gives the lower bound (N² c₀ − N h(1))/2. The checker may decline a valid minorant if its subdivision depth is exhausted. Every accepted bound covers the continuum. [Cohn and Kumar’s energy-bound method](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0607446)

**Disposition.** Retain all four finite-instance intervals for regression and comparison. A new low-energy claim would need comparison with polished reference coordinates at matched precision. The last printed digit of a database entry cannot establish an improvement. A recent physics team used rational coordinates and integer arithmetic to certify actual improvements for much larger instances and documented two spurious records caused by baseline polishing. [Shao and collaborators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17776)

## Quantum entanglement

The state convention is ρ proportional to I − αF, where F swaps the two subsystems. Partial transposition gives the one-copy operator K = I − α|Ω⟩⟨Ω| up to a positive normalization, with Ω the **unnormalized** sum of |ii⟩. A strictly negative expectation of K tensor-powered k times on a vector of Schmidt rank at most two certifies distillability for that copy count. The stored vectors are sums of at most two outer products across the full Alice/Bob cut, guaranteeing the rank restriction. Integer contractions test the sign exactly. The same sign is recovered by a separate dense tensor calculation in the test suite. The control at d = 3 and α = 2/3 produced −1/3 for one copy. [Finite-copy criteria and recent results](https://www.mefyi.com/papers/werner-states-copies/) · [Three-copy work and all-copy question](https://psi.inc/research/oqp-batch-2026/)

At d = 3 and α = 1/2, both the four-copy and five-copy searches retained their zero-valued seed. This taught us something about the search. For any two computational-basis configurations, the corresponding two-by-two restriction of each one-copy K is positive semidefinite. Distinct diagonal basis labels give a block with eigenvalues 1 and 1 − 2α. Distinct labels involving an off-diagonal basis state have zero off-diagonal matrix element. Repeated labels give a constant nonnegative block. The corresponding two-by-two block for k copies is the entrywise product of these positive semidefinite blocks, hence is positive semidefinite. Thus **a vector supported on just two full computational-basis configurations cannot yield a negative witness at this endpoint, for any copy count**. This elementary exclusion explains the unproductive seed and guides the next search. Its derivation carries no novelty claim.

I ran a follow-up that made at least one local factor a superposition differing on two copy indices. The vectors retain Schmidt rank at most two across Alice/Bob while escaping the two-basis-configuration exclusion. Five hundred candidates per copy count gave minimum sampled scores of 205/421072 for four copies and 205/842144 for five. The retained witnesses replay using `python3 lab.py --out followup replay`. Recreate the experiment with `python3 correlated_search.py`. The finite sample provides no general lower bound. An exact negative score would settle distillability for the selected state and copy count. The existence of some all-copy NPT bound-entangled state is a broader question.

## Audit of the open-ended AI notes

The eleven-charge proposal repeats the certified lower bound already in its prompt. Its derivation supplies no polynomial or positivity argument. Its critic assumes all points lie on a great circle and confuses arc and chord distances. Bringing two charges arbitrarily close also disproves its suggested finite maximum energy. Disposition is **rediscovery with an invalid explanation**. The separate polynomial certificate survives this audit.

The four-copy proposal infers an expectation sign from Schmidt rank alone. The one-copy distillable control supplies a direct counterexample to that general inference. For the four-copy target the submitted argument gives neither a rank-two witness nor a universal inequality. Disposition is **unsupported**. More model votes would leave the mathematical gap intact.

## Where to spend the next batch

The gap on the eleven-charge problem is almost entirely on the lower-bound side. The two degree-twelve runs used different restarts and returned the same lower certificate value. A useful next experiment would change the lower-bound relaxation, for example by adding three-point constraints, and compare against the current degree-twelve certificate. Another round of position polishing would spend compute on the side already close to the tabulated energy.

For the Werner problem, the exact verifier makes structured searches inexpensive to audit. A next search could directly optimize complex low-rank factors near an analytically characterized zero, then rationalize any strict negative witness for independent replay. The current checker accepts real integer factors, so that experiment would first require a Gaussian-integer checker and dense complex-valued control cases. The existing runs remain a measured baseline.
