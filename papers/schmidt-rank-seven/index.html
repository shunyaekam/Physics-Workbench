Computational-basis optimality of nonlocal stabilizer entropy for rank-seven spectra and two exceptional amplitudes

Abstract

We prove computational-basis optimality for every rank-seven bipartite pure state on two three-qubit registers. In arbitrary supporting register sizes, we give three full-dimensional rank-seven spectral regions with exact rational certificates. For equal registers of any local dimension d=2nd=2^n, we prove optimality when at least d−2d-2 of the full dd Schmidt amplitudes, counting zeros, coincide. The latter proof uses a two-particle occupation bound and gives an exact rank-15 example. These results use Sierant’s analytic reduction of the local-unitary optimization. The unrestricted conjecture remains open.

Limit of this method.

The factor three in the first condition is sharp for the four dimension-independent row types used here. Assign a four-set weight 1/51/5, 1/81/8 or 1/41/4 according as it contains zero, one or two of the leading two indices. These nonnegative values satisfy every such row, as the checker verifies. For unnormalized t=(R,R,1,1,1,1,1)t=(R,R,1,1,1,1,1) their objective exceeds the coordinate objective by R(R−3)/2R(R-3)/2 when R>3R>3. Normalization multiplies both objectives by the same positive factor. This is an obstruction to the linear relaxation. Physical realizability of these weights is a separate question.

Two exceptional Schmidt amplitudes at arbitrary rank

Let the two registers have equal local dimension d=2nd=2^n. Suppose the entire list of dd Schmidt amplitudes consists of d−2d-2 copies of aa and two entries b,cb,c. The values are nonnegative and (d−2)a2+b2+c2=1(d-2)a^2+b^2+c^2=1. The sorted computational-basis state attains the global optimum for every such spectrum, including zeros and exceptions below the common value. For d≤4d\le4 the source’s rank theorem suffices. Consider d≥8d\ge8.

Let u,vu,v be the orthogonal exceptional eigenvectors of NN. On four-wedges write nu,nvn_u,n_v for the commuting projections testing occupancy of the two modes. The coordinate pair therefore maximizes the two terms in Proposition 2 of [1], and the defect vanishes at that diagonal. Lemma 1 then gives the full two-unitary optimum. Appending extra zero amplitudes generally changes the multiplicity hypothesis, so this argument applies to the stated ambient dimensions.

For example, on two four-qubit registers, raw amplitudes (2,…,2,1,0)(2,\ldots,2,1,0) with fourteen twos give a rank-15 pure state. Its optimal purity is 2576747/35186672576747/3518667, which a direct Pauli sum reproduces. Its last dyadic shell has unequal amplitudes, and its rank exceeds the rank-six theorem in [1]. Two exceptions above the background already give a sorted dyadic staircase; the theorem includes previously covered cases.
