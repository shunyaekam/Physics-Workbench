\begin{abstract}
For $k$ copies of a $d$-dimensional Werner state at the one-copy distillability boundary, we compute the largest computational-basis support on Alice's side for which the full compression is positive semidefinite. For three copies and every $d\ge2$ we derive the exact maximum $\max(8,2d)$. The finite table supplies witnesses and replayable upper-bound certificates. The object being maximized is the cardinality of a set of basis words. This finite calculation leaves distillability by Schmidt-rank-two vectors open.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Reduction and certificates}
Use the unnormalized $\Omega=\sum_i |ii\rangle$ and write $K=I-|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|/2$. The companion manuscript proves that compression of $K^{\otimes k}$ to rows indexed by $S$, with Bob unrestricted, is positive semidefinite exactly when each triple of words in $S$ has an even number of coordinates with three distinct symbols. By independent symbol relabeling in each coordinate one word may be fixed at zero. Two other words are compatible when their triangle with zero has even parity. An anchored clique meets the criterion for all triples: the product of three anchored triangle signs gives the sign of the triangle between their non-anchor vertices.

The search branches on a vertex, considering all cliques which contain it and all which exclude it. At each terminal branch the remaining candidates are partitioned into independent sets. Each clique takes at most one vertex from each set. Adding the included vertices gives the certified upper bound. An explicit clique witnesses the lower bound. Some instances are divided into layers by the maximum Hamming weight $t$ relative to the anchor. A word of weight $t$ can be mapped to $t$ leading ones and trailing zeros while fixing the anchor. Exhausting $t=1,\ldots,k$ covers every nonsingleton support. A reported maximum requires all layers.


\section*{A uniform check for three copies}
For every $d\ge2$ the three-copy maximum is $\max(8,2d)$.
The binary box attains eight. Words $(i,i,z)$ with $z$ in a fixed
two-symbol alphabet attain $2d$. Here is the upper bound directly from
the companion paper's even-triple criterion.

If a coordinate uses at most two symbols, projecting it away preserves
admissibility: lift any three distinct projected words and use that the
removed coordinate cannot contribute an all-distinct triple. Each
projected word has at most two lifts. The two-copy bound $\max(4,d)$
from the companion paper gives the result.

Otherwise every coordinate uses at least three symbols. No two words can
differ at just one position: choosing a third word with a third symbol at
that position would give exactly one all-distinct position. For each
fixed first symbol the other two coordinates therefore form a matching,
viewed as edges between two alphabets. If every matching has size at most
two, there are at most $2d$ words. Fix a larger matching of size $t\ge3$,
with edges $(b_i,c_i)$. For any word outside its fiber, write its edge
as $(u,v)$. The triple with matching edges $i,j$ gives
\[
 u\in\{b_i,b_j\}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad v\in\{c_i,c_j\}.
\]
Membership in all two-subsets of $[t]$ distinguishes each index and
distinguishes it from an absent endpoint. Hence an outside edge using a
matched endpoint would repeat the entire edge, giving two words that
differ only at the first position. All outside edges avoid both sets of
matched endpoints.

Two edges from different outside fibers must now share exactly one
endpoint: test their words against a word in the large fiber. The first
symbols are all distinct, so exactly one of the other two positions must
also be all distinct. If an outside fiber has three or more edges, an
edge in a further fiber could meet at most two of that matching's edges.
Thus there are at most two nonempty fibers overall and at most $2d$ words.
If the largest outside matching has size two, its two edges can be met
simultaneously only by the two cross edges. Neither cross edge can recur
in different fibers. This gives at most $t+4\le\max(8,2d)$ words. If
all outside fibers have size at most one, the bound is $t+d-1\le2d$.
This exhausts the cases.
\section*{Reproducibility and scope}
The auditor rebuilds compatibility from the parity of three Hamming distances, replays each decision with sets, validates every color class, checks prefix coverage, and reconstructs the lower witness. An independent matrix test also checks the physical reduction on every two-copy qutrit support. Run \texttt{python3 continuous.py audit} from the repository root. The accompanying certificates specify every search leaf. The certificates prove finite maxima for this specified basis and endpoint. Priority in the combinatorics or quantum information literature remains unassessed. This is an internally reviewed working paper.

\paragraph{AI contributions and verification.} AI assistance was used to draft the explanation of the support certificates. For each table entry, the auditor checks the deposited lower witness, verifies each color class and confirms that the branches cover the search. The separate matching argument gives $\max(8,2d)$ at three copies, including dimensions outside the table.
