Abstract

We prove the sharp order-one-half Wigner–Husimi inequality on each subspace spanned by vacuum and a fixed photon count n=2,3,4,5n=2,3,4,5. Arbitrary coherences and mixtures are allowed. A positive angular series bounds the coherent amplitude from below. Angular quadrature with a known sign of error bounds Wigner negativity from above. A separate inequality handles states arbitrarily close to vacuum.

Theorem 1. For each n∈{2,3,4,5}n\in\{2,3,4,5\} and every density matrix supported on span⁡{|0⟩,|n⟩}\operatorname{span}\{|0\rangle,|n\rangle\}, one has ∥W∥1≤(∫Qdxdp)2/(8π)\|W\|_1\le(\int\sqrt Q\,dx\,dp)^2/(8\pi) in the convention [x,p]=i[x,p]=i of Gagatsos [1]. Equality holds only at vacuum.
