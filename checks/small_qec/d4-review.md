# Independent audit of the binary [[12,3,4]] check-weight optimum

26 November 2026. The proposed value **six is correct** for general binary stabilizer codes, including degenerate codes. This resolves the interval 6–8 printed in Wei et al., Table 2. The lower bound was already published. The upper certificate is readily obtainable from the published construction in Appendix J. I found no earlier explicit assertion of the value six in the sources examined. The historical novelty of the equality remains unestablished. [Wei et al., Table 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.19848v2#S7.T2) · [Appendix J](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.19848v2#A10)

**Direct witness.** Using a fresh binary Pauli implementation, I checked the nine supplied strings have support six, all 36 pairs commute, and the span has size 512. Independent commuting Hermitian Pauli generators define a nonempty eigenspace of dimension eight. I enumerated the 36, 594, 5,940 and 40,095 unsigned physical Pauli strings of weights one through four. Their counts in the normalizer outside the stabilizer are 0, 0, 0 and 105, proving distance exactly four.

**Independent exact lower-bound check.** Wei et al., Eqs. (113)–(116), apply to the complete stabilizer with no purity assumption. Write A_j for the number of weight-j stabilizers. For rank nine the normalization factor is 1/512. I reconstructed the MacWilliams columns directly as coefficients of (1+3t)^(12−j)(1−t)^j. The shadow columns have the additional sign (−1)^j. Applying Eq. (115) gives equality in the first four MacWilliams rows, inequalities in rows 4 through 12, and nonnegative shadow coordinates. The number of even-weight group elements is 512 or 256. Indeed weight parity is a homomorphism on commuting Pauli strings, since each site with distinct nonidentity letters contributes both an anticommutation and a failure of weight additivity modulo two. Its kernel is the whole group or half of it. I multiplied the supplied dual vectors by independently generated rows using exact fractions, verified all inequality multipliers had the correct sign, and checked all 13 coordinates equal the objective of counting nonidentity group elements of weight at most five. The resulting bounds are 8 and 54/7 in the two parity cases. Either bound rules out nine independent generators with support at most five. No mathematical gap was found. This provides a reproducible certificate for the already tabulated lower bound. [MacWilliams and shadow constraints](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.19848v2#A7.E115)

**Priority and significance.** I exhausted the 255 distinct one-logical-Pauli restrictions of Wei's Appendix J [[12,4,4]] code, and independently ran the same count on Grassl's displayed parent. The parent has 256 logical Pauli classes, including identity. Appending one nonidentity class gives a rank-nine child. Sorting each child's 512 elements by weight and greedily adding independent rows gives its true minimum maximum generator weight. In each parent 120 children attain six, 120 attain seven, and 15 attain eight. Restricting to the paper's explicit recipe of appending a weight-four logical gives 75 eligible classes, of which 60 attain six and 15 attain eight. For Wei's exact parent, adjoining XXIIIIXXIIII yields a basis with supports 4,4,6,6,6,6,6,6,6. Thus a small enumeration in the authors' own stated recipe supplies an attaining example. Grassl's specific archived [[12,3,4]] child yields seven after exhaustive basis minimization. It is important to distinguish that selected child from the numerous other restrictions of the archived parent. ## Separate result for four logical qubits

For [[12,4,4]], the eight supplied generators have weights 6,6,7,7,7,7,7,7. An independent enumeration checks all 28 commutators vanish, the unsigned span has 256 elements, and the nontrivial normalizer contains 0, 0, 0, 225 physical Pauli strings of weights one through four. This proves a genuine distance-four witness with maximum check weight seven. Its full stabilizer has 24 elements of weight seven. The explicitly tabulated Grassl code has only even-weight stabilizers, also reproduced from Grassl's February 2003 quaternary generator matrix, and has minimum maximum generator weight eight. Thus the new witness differs from this old code under qubit permutations and single-qubit Cliffords. [Grassl's 2003 matrix](https://www.markus-grassl.de/QECC/circuits/12_4_4.html) · [QEC Challenge](https://qecchallenge.com/codes/12-4-4.html)

The published lower bound seven in Wei's Table 2 also has a valid rational recheck. Repeating the independent polynomial-column verification above with normalization 1/256, nonpositive inequality multipliers, and objective A_1+...+A_6 gives an upper bound of six in the all-even case and two in the mixed-parity case. Both are below the eight independent generators required. The new example attains seven, which settles the published interval 7–8.
