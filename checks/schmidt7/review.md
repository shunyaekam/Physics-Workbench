It covers every seven-term Schmidt spectrum of a bipartite pure state with three qubits per party. With larger registers it covers the two spectral degeneracies stated in the paper. ## Analytic audit

1. Sierant's Lemma 1 and Corollary 1 bound the two-frame functional by the geometric mean of the two same-frame values in the author's conjugated second-frame convention. Thus a same-frame upper bound suffices. Proposition 2, equations 42–43, gives the spectral term plus two positive overlap terms and a nonpositive defect. Theorem 7 applies with support rank seven and bounds the first overlap by $q c_F(N^2)$, with equality at the coordinate frame. Substitution gives $6d^2q+18d=42d$ and $42dq=168$. The neighbouring overlap theorem, Theorem 8, has a rank-six hypothesis. The new coefficient argument supplies that missing step in the claimed cases. The source's deeper Schur–Weyl and compression theorems are external analytic inputs. [Sierant, Sections III and V](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.13106v2)
2. In the coordinate expression for the projector, distinct planes in a common direction are disjoint. An off-diagonal term in a reduction to two or three particles needs a common spectator, so it vanishes. Every coordinate triple has exactly one plane completion. Every pair has $(d-2)/2$ completions counted as planes. The reduced operators are consequently scalar identities. Exterior-power covariance extends the result to complex frames, including phases and reordering. In dimension eight a seven-frame omits one direction. Subtracting that direction's triple occupation from each complete pair sum gives exactly $3q-q=2q$. For a larger complement the same subtraction no longer computes the pair sum. [Sierant, equation 39](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.13106v2#S5)
3. The five-frame inequality in Corollary 4 permits an arbitrary orthonormal five-frame. Each four-subset of a six-frame lies in two of its five-subframes, giving the factor three. In Proposition 10 the complementary edge avoiding a distinguished vertex corresponds precisely to a four-set containing that vertex. The common normalization is $x_A=\|\Pi u_A\|^2/q$. [Sierant, Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.13106v2#A3)
4. Writing each sorted probability as a sum of nonnegative gaps expands the inequality into 210 monomials. Distinguishing repeated gap slots multiplies both sides by the same positive factorial. Nonnegativity of all $x_A$ licenses the dual's componentwise inequality. Each of the five dimension-specific keys contains an index in each of {1, 2} and {2, 3}. Setting the corresponding pair of gaps to zero as the manuscript prescribes removes all five. Appending stabilizer qubits preserves the purity, embeds each smaller-register rotation in the larger group, and the coordinate state attains the bound in the original registers as well.

## Attempts to falsify the result

- Executed the standard-library replay and Lean 4.19.0 kernel replay. All 210 dual inequalities passed, 205 with dimension-independent rows alone. A missing case and a multiplier changed to 999 in disposable copies both produced the expected rejection. The kernel check covers the finite inequalities. The physical derivation of the rows and their application to the entropy are analytic mathematics.
- Implemented a separate exact six-qubit Pauli evaluator using integer Walsh–Hadamard transforms of Gaussian-integer amplitudes. For amplitudes proportional to $(7,6,5,4,3,2,1)$ it gives the stated purity $134539/214375$. All 24 deterministic trials with four complex two-level rotations split between the two registers had strictly smaller purity. These finite trials check phases, normalization and one generic spectrum.
- Constructed a witness against one tempting extension. On labels 0 through 6, give weight one half to each four-set consisting of labels 0, 1 and an edge of a five-cycle on labels 2 through 6. Set all other weights to zero. This obeys **every dimension-independent row** in the paper. Its total pair weight on 0, 1 is $5/2$, while the coordinate frame has 2. For gap key $(1,2,7,7)$ these yield slot-count coefficients 5 and 4. Hence no nonnegative combination of the current dimension-independent rows can prove that coefficient bound. Realizability of this relaxed vector by a physical frame is unknown. A proof for larger dimensions could instead exploit additional geometry or cross-coefficient inequalities.

## Novelty screen

Sierant v2 explicitly identifies rank seven outside dyadic staircases as beyond its optimality theorems, and distinguishes its rank-seven *compression* theorem from its rank-six *overlap* theorem. A generic unequal seven-term spectrum in dimension eight supplies a case beyond the reported results. Liu and Cui had already proposed the sorted Schmidt reference state. Torre, Franchini and Giampaolo had studied a harmonic expression. The working paper should credit the earlier proposal as well as the source of its proof machinery. Searches for the source title, rank-seven Schmidt spectra and computational-basis optimality did not identify a prior proof of this exact arbitrary-spectrum six-qubit case. This screen cannot establish first discovery.
