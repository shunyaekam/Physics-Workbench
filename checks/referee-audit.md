# Review of the reviews

The local model read the complete coordinate-support manuscript in a fresh context and raised two objections. Its raw output is archived in [referee-response.json](referee-response.json). The response ended at its token limit. The following dispositions address the substance of the objections and add separate checks of our own.

| Objection | Test | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| A positive basis-vector expectation on the two-word set {(0,1),(1,2)} contradicts the odd-triple claim. | This set has only two words. Their fully diagonal sign matrix is the two-by-two all-ones matrix. Every other sector is a positive multiple of a principal submatrix. The dense audit also checks this exact support. | Rejected. The theorem asserts existence of one negative superposition when three distinct words give odd parity. |
| Hamming distance is undefined on a singleton support, or on words outside it. | The sign matrix for a singleton is [1] because a word differs from itself at zero sites. Other words do not index that compression. Hamming distance between 00 and 11 is two. | Rejected. The stated examples have well-defined distances. |
| The sign matrix could miss a negative direction with a mismatched Alice/Bob symbol. | A second implementation builds the entire 81-by-81 physical matrix for two copies, discovers connected blocks from its entries, and checks all 512 row compressions by exact integer elimination. | All cases agree, including 415 indefinite compressions. The sector decomposition in the proof handles arbitrary copy count. |
| An attained negative diagonal block could resolve distillability. | Check the coefficient matrix after undoing the sign switch. Its three diagonal entries are nonzero, so its Schmidt rank is three. | The manuscript confines this witness to the compression question. |
| A passing clique finder might leave part of its search unexplored. | The audit verifies disjoint prefix coverage of the binary decision tree, constructs compatibility by three Hamming distances, verifies the explicit clique, and checks the independent vertex classes on every closed leaf. Corruptions drop a leaf, overlap leaves, forge a clique, delete a color cover, and combine incompatible color vertices. | All five corruptions rejected. Open leaves keep the case incomplete. |
| Fixing an anchor and a second word could silently discard a larger clique. | Coordinate permutations and independent symbol relabeling take any anchor to zero and any farthest word to the canonical representative. The maximum across all farthest-weight layers is required. | Layer records are upper bounds only within their specified layer. |

The analytical claim, reduction to finite search, search certificate, and journal priority are distinct questions. This review covers the first three internally. The search for adjacent literature included Werner partial-trace inequalities, signed balance, ternary trifference, and binary symplectic codes. We found related techniques. We have no independent determination of priority. The two manuscripts should be cited as working drafts until a qualified external reader has reviewed them.
