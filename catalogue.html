The polygon bound for finite-spectrum quantum transport costs

For costs proportional to an energy gap raised to a power above one, the worst chain of levels determines the exact approximation error. Relabeling the levels attains that error. For powers below one, the error is zero, but three distinct levels already rule out a single relabeling.

LaTeX source
Verification

The approximation uses lower exponents between zero and one inclusive of one, with an exact formula for every finite spectrum. A second agent in this project reconstructed the proof. Lean checks the lower bound on chains of arbitrary length and a local crossing step in the upper bound. Lean also checks the global directed-cycle counting argument. The quantum interpretation has a written proof. External peer review remains pending.
